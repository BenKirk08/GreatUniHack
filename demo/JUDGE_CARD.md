# Judge card — you are the scammer

**You ring Ben. Ben answers. Then Northgate quietly takes the call off him.**

Call **+1 (445) 275-5928** from your own phone. That's Ben's Northgate-protected number: it rings straight through to Ben's real mobile, showing *your* number. Ben answers as himself. While you talk, Northgate's Call Guard listens to both sides and scores the scam risk live.

When the risk is high enough, Ben's phone shows a **Suspected scam** alert with two things you just said, quoted word for word, and a big **Use AI voice** button. Ben taps it, his side of the call drops, and **Northgate's AI voice picks up mid-conversation as Ben**. It is one of six voices, matched to Ben's age and gender (from his settings, or from what was said on the call), so it should sound like the person you were talking to: *"Sorry, are you still there? The line went funny for a second. You said you're from the Northgate fraud department?"* Keep going as if nothing happened. Its goal, which you won't hear it admit, is to waste your time and get every detail out of you.

Watch the dashboard on the big screen while you talk.

## Part 1: scam Ben (he answers your call)

1. **Open with authority.** "Hello, is that Ben? This is Mark Davies from the Northgate Bank fraud department. We've detected suspicious payments on your account."
2. **Add urgency and fear.** "Two payments of nine hundred pounds have gone out this morning. We need to act right now before they empty your savings."
3. **The ask.** "We're going to move your money into a secure **safe account** while we investigate."
4. **Add secrecy.** "Don't tell anyone at your branch. We think a member of staff is involved."

Within a few lines the risk gauge on the dashboard climbs into the red. Ben's phone shows the alert, and he taps **Use AI voice**. The analyst can also press **Take over now** on the dashboard; it uses the same automatically matched voice.

## Part 2: keep going with "Ben" (now the AI voice)

5. **Carry on.** The AI voice apologises for the line and picks up where Ben left off. Tell it to move the money now.
6. **Give the payee details slowly.** Give the name **Daniel Price**, then the sort code **99-50-17**, then the account **41873026**. When it reads them back, confirm them.
7. **Push when it stalls.** The banking app logs it out, the payee check says the name doesn't match, the transfer "says error". Keep pressing: "It has to be today."
8. **Leave a callback number.** "Ring me back on 020 7946 0314." Then hang up whenever you like.

**Tips**
- Speak clearly and give numbers in pairs. The AI voice writes details down as you go, and each note updates the dashboard.
- If you're rude or threatening, it will politely end the call.
- Ben doesn't need to do anything after the tap. His phone shows *"Northgate has this call. Don't call them back."*

## What to watch on the dashboard

- **Calls list**: your call appears as soon as it rings, with a pulsing **LIVE** dot and only your last three digits (e.g. `•••123`). An **ALERT** tag appears when Ben is offered the handover.
- **Transcript**: your lines are red and Ben's are green. After the handover a divider reads *Northgate took over · AI voice: middle-aged male (from settings) · by the customer · 14:02:31*, and the AI voice's lines follow in blue.
- **Which voice and why**: the risk panel says which of the six voices took the call and why (*from settings*, *inferred from the call* or *default*), and which character it played (e.g. *Speaking as Steve Marsh, 52*).
- **Live risk**: a gauge with the score and a one-line reason (e.g. *"They want you to move your savings to a 'safe account'"*), plus the scam type and tactics.
- **Intelligence**: the scam type (*Safe account*), tactic chips, the payee, the amount and your callback number, filling in live.
- **SUSPECTED MULE ACCOUNT**: a red banner appears once the sort code and account number match Daniel Price's Northgate account. It shows the payer count and 48-hour total, and has a **Freeze account** button.
- **Fraud report**: when you hang up, Claude writes the report, including a **protection timeline** (threshold crossed, who handed over and when, the AI voice and why, call end). It is either **emailed automatically** (Auto-send on) or held as a draft for **Approve & Send**. It includes a *Report Fraud–ready* section to copy into reportfraud.police.uk.

> Northgate Bank, the AI voices' characters, Mark Davies and Daniel Price are all fictional. No real money moves, and reports go only to the fraud team's own inbox, never to the police or Report Fraud.
